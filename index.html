Lattice IBE

GPV IBE · Dual-LWE · Toy Parameters

Hash an exact identity to a public syndrome, extract its private preimage with a GPV trapdoor, and test real dual-LWE encryption against a public attack on deliberately tiny parameters.

WHY IT MATTERS

Identity-based encryption can let a sender encrypt directly to an address instead of first finding a certificate. The tradeoff is central key escrow: whoever controls the master trapdoor can derive every recipient key, and insecure parameters erase the promised protection.

Mechanism toy, not secure parameters. This page executes GPV's algebra at dimension four. It deliberately fails the paper's asymptotic parameter conditions, and a public bounded search recovers a working key.

Identity → SHA-256 → syndrome

GPV §7.2 models this map as a random oracle. This bounded profile instead uses a named SHA-256 domain and unbiased 16-bit rejection sampling; that is executable, deterministic, and not a proof-preserving random-oracle instantiation.

SampleISIS finds e with A e = u mod q
