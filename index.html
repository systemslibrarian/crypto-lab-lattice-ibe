Lattice IBE

Turn an identity into a public lattice syndrome, use a master trapdoor to sample its private preimage, then encrypt and decrypt one bit with the GPV dual-LWE equations.

Mechanism toy, not secure parameters. This page executes GPV's algebra at dimension four. It deliberately fails the paper's asymptotic parameter conditions, and a public bounded search recovers a working key.

Choose exact identity bytes

The profile accepts 1–96 printable ASCII characters without spaces. It does not normalize case, aliases, or Unicode; those enrollment questions sit outside the construction.

1 · Public address

Identity → SHA-256 → syndrome

GPV §7.2 models this map as a random oracle. This bounded profile instead uses a named SHA-256 domain and unbiased 16-bit rejection sampling; that is executable, deterministic, and not a proof-preserving random-oracle instantiation.

2 · Central extraction

SampleISIS finds e with A e = u mod q

The authority solves for one coset representative, then runs GPV's randomized nearest-plane SampleD over the secret short basis. Repeated extraction returns the cached key, as the stateful extractor in GPV §7.2 requires.

5 · Parameter boundary

          
The theorem hypotheses are not decorative

          

            Exact algebraic invariants can pass while security prerequisites fail. “Post-quantum”
            applies to the source construction under its LWE/SIS assumptions and parameters, not
            automatically to this four-dimensional run.
